HER mechanisms

Reaction in acidic media

Global reaction:
$$\ce{2H+ + 2e- <=> H2}$$
Volmer Step
$$\ce{H+ + M + e- <-->[{k1}][{k1r}] MH}$$
Heyrovsky Step
$$\ce{H+ + MH + e- <-->[{k2}][{k2r}] H2 + M}$$
Tafel Step
$$\ce{2MH <-->[{k3}][{k3r}] H2 + 2M}$$

Reaction in alkaline media

Global reaction:
$$\ce{2H2O + 2e- <=> H2 + 2OH-}$$
Volmer Step
$$\ce{H2O + M + e- <-->[{k1}][{k1r}] MH + OH-}$$
Heyrovsky Step
$$\ce{H2O + MH + e- <-->[{k2}][{k2r}] M + H2 + OH-}$$
Tafel Step
$$\ce{2MH <-->[{k3}][{k3r}] H2 + 2M}$$
Key Parameters

Common model parameters and suggested ranges (useful starting points).

ParameterDescriptionTypical Range
k1Forward rate constant (Volmer)$1\times10^{-12}$ – $1\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$
k1rReverse rate constant (Volmer)$1\times10^{-12}$ – $1\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$
k2Forward rate constant (Heyrovsky)$1\times10^{-12}$ – $1\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$
k2r*Reverse rate constant (Heyrovsky)$1\times10^{-12}$ – $1\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$
k3Foward rate constant (Tafel)$1\times10^{-14}$ – $1\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$
k3r*Reverse rate constant (Tafel)$1\times10^{-14}$ – $1\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{mol\,cm^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$

*k2r and k3r are calculated from the others rate constants in Volmer-Heyrovsky-Tafel model:

$$\ce{k2r = k1 * k2 / k1r}$$ $$\ce{k3r = k1^2 * k3 / k1r^2}$$
